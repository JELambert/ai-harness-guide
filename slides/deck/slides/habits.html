What to take home

Three habits that made both demos work

Explain before write

The claim card came before any analysis. The schema came before any code.

Make failure visible

Row counts, version stamps, and a change report turned a quiet recode into a finding.

Ask for alternatives

Two keys, two estimators: see the tradeoff, then choose. That is the Miller debate in miniature.

Tie each habit back to a specific moment from the demos so it sticks. These are the same three habits taught in BUS 751, which is a nice proof that they transfer from students to faculty work.
