AI Harness Guide

Working with AI tools that can see your files, run your analysis, and show their work.

Joshua E. Lambert

Open with the promise, not the definitions. Everything today runs on one real question from political science: are coups good for democracy? By the end we will have read the paper, pulled its replication data, reproduced a figure, caught the data changing under us, and turned it into a small database that keeps itself current. No installation is needed to follow along.
