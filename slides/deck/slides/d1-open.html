Demo 1 · Paper to data · 30 minutes

Are coups good for democracy?

Derpanopoulos, Frantz, Geddes and Wright · Research & Politics 3(1), 2016 · open access

The question: can a harness take us from a citation to a checked number in one sitting?

Why this paper: it is open access, it has a public replication archive on Harvard Dataverse, it uses the Powell and Thyne coup dataset that is still updated today, and it started a published debate. That makes it ideal for showing what a harness does well and where you still have to think.
