Step 1 · Scope

Start with the module card

One short page, the same shape for every module. The harness builds from it; you review against it.

# coups

Contract: Signal. Owns store/coups.db.

Gives you: every coup attempt since 1950, kept current, plus new research on each case.

You provide: nothing. Both sources are open.

Agent builds: a fetcher, three tables, a version check, a freshness alarm.

Command: coups sync · coups ask

This card follows the Field Guide’s module shape: what it gives you, what you provide, what your agent builds, and the command it adds. Writing the card first is the scope step; it is also what you check the result against.
