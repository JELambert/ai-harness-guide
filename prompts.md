# Prompts from the session

Open Claude Code or Codex in this folder and paste any of these. They work on the sample files in `semester/`. Swap in your own files when you are ready.

Run `semester/research/get-paper.sh` once first to download the reading for prompt 4.

## 0. Get oriented

> Read this folder and the brief. Tell me what is here, what you would be able to help me with, and what you are not allowed to do.

## 1. An email

> Read the email from Pat in semester/inbox. List every request and its deadline in a note, then draft a reply confirming them. Don't send anything.

## 2. A syllabus

> Update last spring's syllabus in semester/teaching for spring 2027, using the academic calendar and what Pat asked for in the email. Save it as a Word document in semester/notes. Flag anything I need to decide instead of deciding it for me.

## 3. A paper

> Write a reading note on the paper in semester/research for my students: the question, the method, the main finding with its page number, and one limitation. Link it to the week it fits in the new syllabus.

## 4. A spreadsheet

> Summarize the survey in semester/data for the curriculum committee: one paragraph and one chart. Tell me what you cleaned before you counted, and how many responses were left.

## 5. Put it together

> What do I owe whom this week, and what is ready? Point to the file for each item.

## Habits worth keeping

- **Explain before you write:** "Before changing anything, tell me what you found and what you plan to do."
- **Ask for a check:** "Check every date against the calendar and show me any that do not match."
- **Ask for pushback:** "What am I missing? What would make this wrong?"
- **Keep it in the brief:** "Add that to AGENTS.md so I don't have to say it again."
- **Use the undo button:** "Commit this with a short message." "Undo what you just did."
