MGMT 340. Data-Driven Decision Making. 3 credit hours.
Prerequisites: MATH 202 or STAT 210, and junior standing.
Using data to frame, analyze, and communicate managerial decisions. Topics include
problem framing, descriptive analytics, experiments and A/B testing, regression for
decision support, forecasting, dashboards and data storytelling, and the ethical and
organizational implications of analytics and automation.
Student learning outcomes:
1. Frame a business problem as a question that data can answer.
2. Summarize and visualize data accurately for a managerial audience.
3. Interpret the results of a simple experiment and a regression model.
4. Communicate a data-supported recommendation in writing and in person.
5. Evaluate the ethical and organizational consequences of analytics and automation.
